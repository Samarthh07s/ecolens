The project

{{ APP_NAME }} turns live environmental data into one clear answer: how healthy is it outside right now, and what should I do about it? It combines air quality, temperature, humidity, UV and (optionally) noise into an Environmental Health Score, detects risky patterns, gives profile-specific advice (children, elderly, asthma, athletes), stores history for trend analysis and raises alerts when thresholds are crossed.

The "AI" is a transparent rule-based expert system: weighted scoring, threshold bands from US EPA and WHO, pattern rules and a small statistical model (linear regression) for short-term estimates. Every number can be traced back to a rule shown in the app.

Data sources

  • Open-Meteo Forecast API – temperature, humidity, apparent temperature, UV index, wind (hourly, past 2 days + next 2 days).
  • Open-Meteo Air Quality API – US AQI, PM2.5, PM10 from the CAMS atmospheric model.
  • Open-Meteo Geocoding API – city search and autocomplete.
  • Your device – GPS location (optional) and microphone for noise (optional, never recorded or uploaded; only the dB number is saved).

Limitations

  • Noise is approximate. Browser microphones are not calibrated and apply their own processing; values can be off by 10 dB or more. Treat it as an indicator, not a measurement.
  • AQI is model-based. It comes from the CAMS atmospheric model on a ~10–40 km grid, not a street-level sensor. Local sources (traffic, construction, burning) may make your spot worse.
  • The 6-hour estimate is a straight-line extrapolation and ignores daily cycles.
  • Health advice is general guidance, not medical advice.

Future scope

  • IoT sensors – ESP32 with PM2.5 (PMS5003), DHT22 and a calibrated sound sensor for hyper-local readings.
  • ML prediction – train a time-series model (e.g. gradient boosting or LSTM) on stored history for real AQI forecasts.
  • Mobile app – a PWA or Flutter app with home-screen widgets.
  • Location-based alerts – background push alerts for saved places (home, school, office).
