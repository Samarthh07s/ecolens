        How the score is calculated

        Each measurement is converted to a sub-score from 0 (worst) to 100 (best) using the threshold bands below. The overall score is a weighted average:

        FactorWeightIdeal range
        Air quality (US AQI){{ (method.weights.aqi * 100)|round|int }}%0 – 50
        Heat index{{ (method.weights.heat_index * 100)|round|int }}%18 – 27 °C
        UV index{{ (method.weights.uv * 100)|round|int }}%0 – 2
        Humidity{{ (method.weights.humidity * 100)|round|int }}%30 – 60 %
        Noise{{ (method.weights.noise * 100)|round|int }}%< 40 dB

        Score = Σ (sub-score × weight). If noise has not been measured, it is left out and the other four weights are scaled up so they still add to 100%. Weakest-link rule: the score can be at most {{ method.weakest_link_margin }} points above the worst of the air-quality, heat and UV sub-scores, so one dangerous factor (e.g. AQI 300) cannot be hidden by good values elsewhere.

        Pattern rules

        • Heat stress: temperature ≥ 30 °C with humidity ≥ 60 %, or heat index ≥ 32 °C.
        • Stagnant pollution: PM2.5 > 35.4 µg/m³ with wind < 10 km/h.
        • Midday sun: UV index ≥ 6.
        • Harmful noise: noise > 80 dB.
